The details that change a result

Hubs are ranked before cable cost. Retraces are allowed and counted as overlap. Reversals count as one bend; turns at nodes and the closing hub also count. Crossings are counted once per point, including between different rings.

Coordinates keep the input origin and are divided by scale. Fixed nodes never move. The default 0.5 m wire clearance is your stricter requirement; the supplied slides specify that distance for nodes and hubs. Narrow doors may therefore require more hubs.

J is a recomputed objective, not a competition rank or a physical energy unit. Hidden-plan rank points and final judge weights are unavailable. Search is heuristic for cable cost.
